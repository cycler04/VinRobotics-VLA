# Retry / Recovery in VLA — retained paper index

**Scope (31 July 2026; extended 3 August 2026):** this directory contains the six
primary PDFs below. Venue/status is recorded from the PDF itself; where the PDF
states no venue the entry says so rather than guessing. Citation counts are
omitted because they change over time.

| # | Paper | Source | Local PDF | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | RoboMonkey: Scaling Test-Time Sampling and Verification for Vision-Language-Action Models | [arXiv 2506.17811](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.17811) | `01_robomonkey_test_time_sampling_verification.pdf` | Venue not stated in the retained PDF |
| 2 | FailSafe: Reasoning and Recovery from Failures in Vision-Language-Action Models | [arXiv 2510.01642](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01642) | `02_failsafe_reasoning_recovery_from_failures.pdf` | Venue not stated in the retained PDF (IEEE two-column format) |
| 3 | Self-Correcting VLA: Online Action Refinement via Sparse World Imagination | [arXiv 2602.21633](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21633) | `03_self_correcting_vla_sparse_world_imagination.pdf` | Preprint |
| 4 | FLARE: A Failure-Aware Framework for Autonomous Correction and Recovery in Visual-Language Robotic Manipulation | [CVF Open Access](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/papers/Zhao_FLARE_A_Failure-Aware_Framework_for_Autonomous_Correction_and_Recovery_in_CVPR_2026_paper.pdf) | `04_flare_failure_aware_correction_recovery.pdf` | CVPR 2026 |
| 5 | NORA-1.5: A Vision-Language-Action Model Trained using World Model- and Action-based Preference Rewards | [arXiv 2511.14659](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.14659) | `05_nora_1_5_world_model_preference_rewards.pdf` | arXiv preprint (v1, 18 Nov 2025); no venue stated in the retained PDF |
| 6 | ThinkAct: Vision-Language-Action Reasoning via Reinforced Visual Latent Planning | [arXiv 2507.16815](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.16815) | `06_thinkact_reinforced_visual_latent_planning.pdf` | arXiv preprint (v2, 18 Sep 2025); no venue stated in the retained PDF |

Note: a different paper also named FLARE — *FLARE: Robot Learning with Implicit
World Modeling* ([arXiv 2505.15659](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15659)) — is cited
inside paper #3. It is a world-model paper, unrelated to retry/recovery, and is
not part of this corpus.

`deep-research-report.md` in this directory is a broader survey retained from
earlier work. It covers papers with no PDF here and citation counts that were not
re-verified; it is not the canonical index.

Papers #5 and #6 were added on 3 August 2026. Neither is a retry system: NORA-1.5
is offline preference post-training and ThinkAct is a reasoning architecture whose
self-correction evidence is qualitative only. Both are retained because they sit
on the same reliability axis; the classification caveats are in the Vietnamese
reports.

The six Markdown analyses are indexed in
[`docs/research_finding/06-retry-handle/01_tong_quan.md`](../../research_finding/06-retry-handle/01_tong_quan.md).
